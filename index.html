About the Project

This project demonstrates how AI-style decision rules can estimate whether water is likely Safe or Unsafe to drink based on measurable parameters.

pH

pH measures how acidic or basic water is. Drinking water is typically acceptable when pH is between 6.5 and 8.5.

Turbidity

Turbidity refers to how clear the water is. Lower values indicate clearer water. A value below 5 NTU is often used as a guideline for safe drinking water.

Hardness

Hardness is caused by dissolved minerals (like calcium and magnesium). It affects taste and scaling but is less critical than pH and turbidity for immediate potability.

How AI is used

In a full AI solution, a trained Machine Learning model would learn patterns from real water-quality datasets and make predictions. In this demo, we use a simple rule-based logic that mimics a model’s decision: if pH is between 6.5–8.5 and turbidity < 5, the water is considered Safe; otherwise Unsafe.
